# Seed archive fixtures from a test account

Cyd Mobile produces its real-data Cyd Bluesky archive fixtures by exporting a curated Bluesky test account through its own version 2 writer, and commits the resulting complete and incomplete archives for later reader work. Such fixtures carry real facets, CIDs, media bytes, and identifiers that synthetic generation does not reproduce, and they need no separate collection tooling, because reading Bluesky saved data is already the writer's job. They demonstrate Cyd Mobile's behavior rather than the contract's meaning: the pinned canonical bundle remains the semantic oracle, and a fixture Cyd Mobile generated never settles a question about what Bluesky version 2 requires. Fixtures stay small by curating the test account before export rather than by trimming the archive afterward, since committed media persists in repository history.
